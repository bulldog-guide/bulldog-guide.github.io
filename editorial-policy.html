Our Editorial Standards

English Bulldogs are a breed with well-documented health vulnerabilities. Bulldog Guide holds its content to standards that reflect the seriousness of those vulnerabilities. This page explains what those standards are.

Breed-Specific Accuracy as the First Test

The Bulldog Guide Research Desk applies a specific filter to every claim before it is published: does this hold true for English Bulldogs specifically? The breed has physiological traits — brachycephalic airways, heat regulation challenges, skin fold anatomy — that mean generic dog advice is often wrong or irrelevant when applied to them. Guides that would work for a Labrador may be actively misleading for a bulldog owner. We check for this at every stage.

How Sources Are Used

The Research Desk draws on published veterinary literature, breed health studies (including data from the UK Kennel Club and Orthopaedic Foundation for Animals), and well-established guidance from practitioners with brachycephalic breed experience. Where statistics are cited, the source type is considered before inclusion. We do not cite individual forum posts or social media anecdotes as evidence for health claims.

The Review Stage

All content goes through an editorial review before publication. The reviewer checks for breed-specific accuracy, logical consistency, and whether the guide is actionable for a real bulldog owner in a real situation. Articles covering breathing, heat, and skin fold hygiene — the three areas where wrong information can cause direct harm — receive the most thorough review.

Where AI drafting tools have been used, the output is treated as a first draft only. The Research Desk rewrites, corrects, and supplements before anything is considered ready to publish.

Update Policy

Guides are updated when: (a) new evidence changes the picture, (b) a reader identifies a factual error, or (c) a recommendation becomes outdated. Updates are made to the existing page and logged with an update note where the change is material. We do not remove content that turned out to be wrong without explaining the correction.

Commercial Independence

The Research Desk's editorial judgements are not available for purchase. Affiliate links are used — see the How This Site Is Funded page — but their presence does not influence what the guide says about any product or topic. If a sponsored content request arrived at Bulldog Guide, it would be declined. The site's reputation rests on accuracy, not on advertiser satisfaction.
