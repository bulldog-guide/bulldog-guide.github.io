The fitting room / no sign-up

A harness size, from your measurements.

Chest and neck can land in different sizes. Compare both against a manufacturer's chart, one size at a time. No universal S/M/L chart, no product ranking.

Changing units converts values already entered.
01 / Your bulldog

Take the chest circumference around the widest part behind the front legs. Take the neck circumference where the chosen model's chart specifies. Use the same measuring points as that chart.

02 / A size from your chosen chart
