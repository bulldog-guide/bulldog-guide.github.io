Why the English Bulldog Annual Cost Per Year Is Higher: 5 Facts You Need

The English Bulldog annual cost per year facts explain why this breed requires a larger budget. Below are the main drivers of cost — each tied to a safety or health risk.

  1. Brachycephalic airway syndrome — surgeries can cost $2,000–$6,000. English Bulldog annual cost per year history shows this is the top expense.
  2. Skin fold infections — chronic dermatitis needs specialized wipes, creams, and vet visits.
  3. Dental disease — bulldogs have crowded teeth; professional cleaning costs $300–$900 yearly.
  4. Higher insurance premiums — pet insurance for bulldogs can be 2x more than for mixed breeds.
  5. Joint & mobility issues — supplements and possible hip surgery (ACL tears).

According to Veterinary Practice News, bulldogs are 3–4 times more likely to require surgery than the average dog. This is why a safety-first budget is not optional.
