About This Guide

Bulldog Guide was built to give English Bulldog owners honest, practical information — no fluff, no inflated promises. This breed has real medical needs, real breed-specific vulnerabilities, and owners deserve content that acknowledges both.

Why This Guide Exists

English Bulldogs are one of the most misunderstood breeds when it comes to care guides. A lot of advice floating around was written for generic dogs and lightly adapted to bulldogs — which means it glosses over the things that matter most: heat intolerance, brachycephalic airway syndrome, skin fold hygiene, and joint strain. Bulldog Guide was created specifically to cover those areas with the seriousness they deserve.

The Bulldog Guide Research Desk approaches content the way a careful owner approaches a vet visit: you come prepared, you ask the right questions, and you don't accept a vague answer when a specific one is available. That same standard applies to what we publish.

Who Produces This Content

Content on this site is produced by the Bulldog Guide Research Desk — an editorial team rather than a single author. We don't publish under individual names, and we don't manufacture credentials. What the Research Desk brings is careful attention to whether published claims hold up for this breed in particular.

Before an article is published, someone on the team reviews it specifically for breed accuracy. "Can bulldogs do X?" has a different answer than "Can most dogs do X?" — and the Research Desk keeps that distinction front and center.

Topics We Cover

Bulldog Guide focuses on the care decisions that come up most for English Bulldog owners:

  • Heat safety and recognizing overheating early
  • Skin fold and wrinkle care routines
  • Breathing — when it's normal, when it's not
  • Stair safety, joint health, and weight management
  • Feeding guidance for a breed that gains weight easily
  • Seasonal concerns: summer heat, winter cold, indoor humidity

What We Won't Do

We won't pretend bulldog health problems don't exist to make the breed sound more appealing. Potential owners deserve accurate information about what they're getting into. We won't invent testimonials or manufacture a fake editorial staff. The team is small, the information is as accurate as we can make it, and the goal is utility — not entertainment.

Funding and Independence

Bulldog Guide uses affiliate links — primarily through Amazon — to support the cost of running the site. There's a full explanation on the How This Site Is Funded page. Affiliate links do not shape which topics we cover or how we cover them. The Research Desk operates independently from any revenue the site generates.

For details on how we research and publish, see our Editorial Standards. To reach us with corrections or questions, visit the Contact page.
