Why English Bulldogs Have the Highest C‑Section Rate

English bulldogs are famous for their wide chests, massive heads, and sturdy bodies — traits that make natural delivery difficult. The english bulldog c section rate is estimated between 80% and 95% globally. That means 8 or 9 out of 10 litters are delivered via cesarean. This isn’t a flaw; it’s the result of breed history. Over the last century, breeders selected for a distinct look, and the maternal anatomy changed. For the safest outcome, planned c‑sections are now standard.

History & Facts — Why the Rate Exists

By the 1990s, veterinarians recognised that >80% of bulldog births required surgical help.
