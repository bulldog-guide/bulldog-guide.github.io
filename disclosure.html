How This Site Is Funded

Bulldog Guide earns income through the Amazon Associates affiliate program. This page explains how that works and what it means — and what it does not mean — for the guides you read here.

Amazon Affiliate Links

Some product links on this site are Amazon affiliate links. When a visitor clicks one of these links and completes a qualifying purchase, Bulldog Guide receives a percentage of the sale from Amazon. This is a standard revenue model for content sites. The price you see on Amazon is the same whether you arrive via an affiliate link or by going directly to Amazon.co.uk or Amazon.com.

How Products Get Mentioned

Products are mentioned in guides because they are relevant to the topic — not because an affiliate link exists for them. The Research Desk selects and reviews content based on what is useful for English Bulldog owners. Whether a given product has an affiliate link does not raise or lower its chances of being included in a guide.

Bulldog Guide does not accept payment from product manufacturers or brands in exchange for mentions. No advertiser pays for a positive review, a product inclusion, or a favourable description. Affiliate income is earned from Amazon based on purchases, not on relationships with specific brands.

FTC Disclosure

This disclosure meets the requirements of the United States Federal Trade Commission's guidelines on affiliate and endorsement disclosures (16 CFR Part 255). Bulldog Guide is a participant in the Amazon Associates Programme, an affiliate advertising programme designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by advertising and linking to Amazon.

No Paid Editorial Content

There is no sponsored content on Bulldog Guide. The Research Desk does not produce paid articles, advertorials, or commercially influenced reviews. All content decisions are made on editorial grounds only.
