Contact the Research Desk

Bulldog Guide takes accuracy seriously, which means corrections matter. If you've spotted an error — a claim that doesn't hold up for English Bulldogs specifically, a recommendation that has been superseded, or a statistic that looks wrong — please reach out. The Research Desk will review it.

We also welcome topic suggestions. If there's a bulldog health or care question you can't find a straightforward answer to, that's exactly the kind of gap we want to fill.

How to Reach Us

Direct your message to: hello@bulldog-guide.github.io

For corrections, please include the URL of the article in question and the specific claim you're questioning. The clearer the message, the faster we can act on it. Bulldog health topics — especially anything related to breathing, heat, or skin — are prioritised for review.

Partnership and advertising enquiries are not considered via this address.
